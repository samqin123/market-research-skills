Where the 2026 Policy Window Opens

A one-page sample rendered from the classic-blue template

Bottom line: the next twelve months are a sequencing problem, not a demand problem. Three policy gates decide whether the current capex cycle extends into 2027.

1. The base case is a soft landing with a narrow runway

Headline demand is holding, but the composition has shifted from volume to replacement. That distinction matters because replacement cycles are shorter and more sensitive to financing costs. The same aggregate number can therefore hide two very different 2027 outcomes.

We treat the official statistics as facts, consensus estimates as estimates, and our own scenario weights as inferences. The three states are labelled separately throughout the underlying model; none of the inference is presented as observed data.

3. What would change our mind

A sustained widening of the credit spread and a simultaneous downward revision to the investment deflator would invalidate the base case. We would then move the scenario weight rather than rewrite the facts.
